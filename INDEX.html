About

My mission is to deliver high‑quality product, food, lifestyle, and portrait photography for clients who value clean, expressive visuals. I focus on technical precision, strong colour, and clear creative direction. My bilingual and cultural sensitivity helps me create images that feel both professional and emotionally engaging.

Biography

I am a Colombian photographer based in Toronto, specializing in fashion, beauty, and product photography. My work blends a clean commercial style with a strong sense of storytelling, influenced by my early passion for nature and wildlife imagery. I focus on creating modern, expressive visuals that help people and brands stand out.

Along with my commercial work, I also photograph portraits and special events, bringing the same attention to detail, lighting, and direction to every project. I volunteer my photography skills to local animal rescues as well, supporting their efforts to help animals find safe and loving homes.

Clients describe me as reliable and easy to work with. I have a strong understanding of lighting, experience in retouching and post‑production, and I’m comfortable directing models and talent to bring out natural, confident performances. On set, I’m a creative problem‑solver who adapts quickly and keeps the process smooth from start to finish.

I work in both English and Spanish and enjoy collaborating with clients from different cultures and industries, whether in studio or on location.
